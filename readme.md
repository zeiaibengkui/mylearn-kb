## About

Hi, my name is Li Chunlai — Chunlai is my first name, Li my last. I spend most
of my free time on two things: algorithmic problem solving and learning machine
learning from the ground up. This site is where both accumulate.

## What's here

- **Problem notes** — mostly [Luogu](https://www.luogu.com.cn) problems, each
  with the statement and C++ solutions validated against the samples before
  they were saved;
- **OI notes** — topic write-ups on data structures, number theory, graph
  theory, and friends;
- **Machine learning notes** — [my study notes](notes/ML/index.md), from the
  math basics up to Transformers and reinforcement learning;
- **Essays and miscellany** — whatever I feel like writing down.
