# Natural Scroll or Reverse Scroll

Imagine a seen: You're reading an article and swipped your touchpad to scroll
down, but the texts moved up! This isn't such a matter, but annoying frankly.

I've been using both methods for a long time. Here I'll provide some insights on it.

`Natural` means that the content scrolls naturally the same direction as your fingers move while `Reverse` is the opposite direction. But I think it's rather scrolling the viewport.

It is confusing that the screen and the touchpad are usually vertical, then what
is "as your finger's direction"? Actually, in most times, we suppose that the screen and
the touchpad are in a shape like `_/` or even `_._` where natural scroll seems
to be obvious like you are dragging the content.

Some people uses their left hand to write while the others not. We should
understand and respect others' custom and choice.
